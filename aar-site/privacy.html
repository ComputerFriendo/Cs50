Privacy

Short version: this site does not collect your AAR project. It stays in your browser, unencrypted, until you delete it or clear your browser data.

Do not enter sensitive information. Do not enter classified, restricted, confidential, privileged, sensitive personal, protected health, or otherwise sensitive information. This tool is intended for general, non-sensitive AAR compilation. Your project data is stored locally in your browser and is not encrypted by this website.

What this site is

  • A static website: a set of fixed files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) served by the hosting provider, Neocities. There is no application server and no database behind it.
  • There are no user accounts, logins or passwords.

What happens to your AAR project

  • The compiler stores your project in your browser's localStorage under one key named aarc.v1. It is never sent to this website or to anyone else.
  • LocalStorage is not encrypted. Anyone who can use your browser profile can read it, and browser extensions with broad permissions may be able to as well.
  • Your project can be lost if you clear browser data, use private browsing, or switch browsers or devices. Download regular JSON backups from Stage 17.
  • Files you export (HTML, Markdown, text, CSV, JSON) are saved by your browser to your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Imported backup files are read inside your browser and are not uploaded.
  • Participant contact details, if you choose to enter them, are left out of every report and section export. They remain in your JSON backup.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, session recording or fingerprinting scripts are included in these files.
  • No cookies are set by these files.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or images are loaded. The pages' security policy blocks network connections from scripts entirely.
  • Site search runs in your browser. Search terms are not sent anywhere.

What the hosting provider may see

Like any web host, Neocities receives standard request information when your browser downloads these pages (for example your IP address and browser type). That is governed by Neocities' own policies, not this site's. This site has not verified what Neocities logs.

External links

Official resources and report links lead to third-party websites, mostly government agencies. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or analytics. This site does not control them. External links open in a new tab and are marked with ↗ and the destination's name.

Shared and public computers

Avoid using the compiler on shared or public computers. If you must, download a backup and then use Reset project in Stage 17 before you leave, and close the browser.

Changes

If the site's behaviour changes (for example if analytics were ever considered), this page will be updated first. Any such change would be reviewed separately for privacy and necessity.
